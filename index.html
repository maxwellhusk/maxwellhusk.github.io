Summary

Karaoke Studio processes songs, lyrics, settings, and recordings on your device. Vocal separation and speech recognition run on-device. If you use online search, lyric lookup, song download, shared timing, or Smule upload features, information needed for that feature is sent to the relevant third-party service. The app does not operate an account system or its own analytics or advertising service.

Information processed on your device

The app can process audio files you import or download, song titles and related metadata, lyrics, vocal-separation output, speech-recognition results, app settings, and audio or video recordings you choose to make. These items are stored in the app's storage on your device. Some downloaded songs and exported recordings may also be saved in shared folders such as Music/Karaoke and Music/Karaoke/Recordings, where other apps with appropriate access may see them.

Microphone access is used when you choose to record audio. Camera access is used only when you choose to record video. Vocal separation and speech recognition process the selected audio locally; the app does not send audio to a developer-operated server for those operations.

Information sent to third-party services

Network features are optional, but they require an internet connection. When you use them, the service provider may receive your IP address, request details, and other information described in that provider's privacy policy.

  • YouTube and Google services: Search terms, links, or video identifiers may be sent when you search for songs, request suggestions, view song information, or download a selected track. YouTube or Google may also receive requests for thumbnails and media. Use of these features is subject to the providers' terms and policies.
  • Spotify: If you import a Spotify playlist or album, the app requests the linked playlist information from Spotify so it can show track names. The app does not request your Spotify password.
  • LRCLIB and lyrics.ovh: Song title, artist, and, where relevant, album or duration are sent to search for matching lyrics and timing. Retrieved lyrics are saved locally with the song.
  • Smule: If you choose the Smule upload feature, the selected audio and the arrangement information you provide, such as title, artist, lyrics, timing, tags, and cover information, are sent to Smule. You may need to sign in to Smule in its page displayed by the app. Smule handles your account and uploaded content under its own policies.
  • GitHub and model hosts: The app may request shared lyric-timing references from GitHub and download vocal or speech models from GitHub or Hugging Face. These downloads do not include your selected song audio. The current Play build does not transmit diagnostic reports to a developer-operated service.

Sharing and sale

The publisher does not sell personal information. The app does not send your local song library or recordings to the publisher. Information is sent to third parties only when needed for a feature you choose, as described above. Third-party services process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Song files, lyrics, settings, models, and recordings kept in app storage remain there until you delete them in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Items exported to shared device storage are not necessarily removed when you uninstall; delete those separately using your device or another app. Content uploaded to Smule is managed through Smule and may remain there according to your Smule account settings and Smule's policies.

Security

On-device files are protected by Android's app storage controls. Network connections to the services used by the app use HTTPS where supported. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and third-party services have their own security practices.

Children

Karaoke Studio is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the publisher, contact us using the address above so we can review the request.
